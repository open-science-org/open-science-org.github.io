The problem

What builds on what

Stars are the targets, with bounties. Dots are the steps of one known proof (ours, hidden from the labs). A step lights up in a lab's colour once a lemma of that lab gives it, checked by Lean; the labs may take other routes, so a grey step means "not proved in this form", and their lemmas off our route (other routes, other forms, or helpers) sit underneath, in the column of their own depth. Lines run from a result to the proofs that use it; depth grows left to right, and the bounty doubles at each step. One panel per setup, all at the same round. Step through the rounds, or press play.
